# Sowing as a directly articulated agricultural process - 2026-10-16

## Approved scope and meaning

The owner approved directly articulating the few clear agricultural processes,
starting with Sowing. Sowing is an agricultural activity deliberately placing seed
into or onto a growing substrate. Its meaning accommodates different equipment,
spatial scales and means of placement. Spontaneous dispersal, storage, germination
and transplanting an already growing plant have different meanings.

Sowing specializes physical:Activity, the agent-performed process head. It does
not specialize physical:Transformation, whose meaning is endogenous transformation.
The declaration has no explicit abstract keyword. Sowing is a concrete process
universal; physical:Activity and imod:Process retain their independent explicit
abstractness. A concrete process concept does not itself instantiate an occurrence
or supply its participants, conditions or dynamics.

## Germination and modeled occurrence

Seed placement does not guarantee successful germination, a cultivated individual,
crop establishment, yield or harvest. Germination can be observed as an event
engendered by sowing; its instantiator model determines whether it occurs under
the particular conditions. A causal description and the occurrence of the
described event are distinct commitments.

The current observable grammar provides `caused by` and `causing` forms
(Observable.xtext, ConceptConnector). "Engendered" is explanatory language, not
a new keyword. This addition declares neither Germination nor a formal causal
connection. Any later causal expression requires a reviewed target and the
appropriate model. No guaranteed effect, soil disturbance, mandatory participant,
bounded episode or automatic attribute conferral is added here.

## Separate crop classification proposal

Crop classification identifies a crop within a classification scheme. It does
not name the sowing operation, provide a taxonomic identity, or identify a later
harvested product. The proposed ICC bridge remains separate until its bearer and
concreteness contract are settled. This commit adds no identity bridge, authority
binding, authority configuration or external classification lookup.

The earlier SowingIdentity/generic-process design remains superseded as the
default for this operation. The agricultural bootstrap is research material;
its other proposed operations, episodes and effects are not promoted by this
small addition.
