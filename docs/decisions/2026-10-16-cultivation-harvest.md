# Cultivation and harvested material - 2026-10-16

## Approved cross-sector placement and contracts

The owner approved placing Cultivated and Harvested in Life so that agriculture,
forestry and wild harvest can reuse the same biological provenance meanings.
No agriculture-only or cultivated-production restriction is added to Harvested.

- Cultivated: "Having been deliberately established or managed through cultivation."
  Applies initially to life:Individual only.
- Harvested: "Having been obtained as a portion of biological material through harvesting."
  Specializes life:OfBiologicalOrigin and applies to physical:MaterialBody.

Harvested refines the existing origin predicate, rather than creating a new
HarvestedBiomass material class. A Harvested MaterialBody description therefore
expresses biological origin; the broad bearer bound does not assert that every
MaterialBody is biological. It does not qualify a living Individual or a grouping.
Cultivated is not widened to land, fields, Assemblage or CollectiveAgent by analogy.
Harvested does not specialize Cultivated or imply prior cultivation.

## Intended meaning and examples

Cultivated characterizes deliberate establishment/management beyond a momentary
process-participation role. A living cultivated plant is distinct from its field,
a stand and any material later obtained from it. Harvested grain, fruit and timber
share material provenance without requiring their source organisms to have been
cultivated; wild-picked fruit is a useful contrast. Naturally fallen biological
material is not automatically Harvested.

Process origin and intended destination can characterize why an entity exists or
is maintained. Intended destination remains distinct from actual use, successful
delivery and guaranteed outcomes: timber intended for construction may later be
used as fuel. Neither attribute confers food/feed/fuel/economic roles, dispositions
or automatic effects. Viability and biological death are separate from harvest.
An Agent is not retyped as a Subject merely because material is harvested.

The identified material portion and its measured Mass remain distinct. Pool
boundaries, inclusion criteria, units and fresh/dry or other basis belong to the
measurement. Crop, taxonomic and product classification identities retain separate
scope. No new classification bridge, code, hierarchy or mandatory identity is added.
