# Fluid parcels and occurrence-specific quantities - 2026-10-18

## Approved observational meaning

physics:FluidParcel specializes physical:MaterialBody as an individuated portion
of fluid delimited by a material-following boundary. A portion of water moving
through an inlet is the positive example; a fixed sampling region through which
successive water portions pass is a different observational target. FluidParcel
is a Subject, not an Agent, geographic Location, Configuration or process.

Observations establish which material defines a particular parcel and its
boundary convention. Models describe motion, mixing, diffusion and exchange and
establish continuity under those conditions. Mixing is not given a universal
identity-loss or new-body creation rule. No fixed material-membership duration,
geometry, scale, marking method or tracking computation is imposed.

The bootstrap physics:FluidParcel entry supplies the research address and
material-following example. Its historical missing-MaterialBody blocker is now
closed: MaterialBody is an established abstract Subject head. This addition uses
that approved head directly and does not add a generic FluidBody intermediary,
a fluid-classification authority or a mandatory identity requirement.

The new named specialization has no explicit abstract keyword. Its boundary
criterion supplies a specific reading while MaterialBody retains its independent
explicit abstract status. A concrete concept is still a universal; it does not
instantiate a particular parcel or guarantee that any model can track it.

## Owner's narrative guidance on quantities

The owner approved the parcel articulation and directed attention to quantities
created by occurrents, chiefly processes and also functional relationships. Such
quantities are not initialized as already existing body states; they arise during
the occurrence or relationship that establishes their meaning. An amount moved
in a transfer is an example of an occurrence-specific observation.

This guidance remains narrative. A parcel may already have observable mass,
volume and temperature before a particular transfer begins; those quantities
can be observed independently and may be affected during transport. The parcel
concept does not make every body quantity occurrence-created. Initial existence,
occurrence-specific meaning and numerical initialization must not be conflated
with a missing measurement or an assumed zero.

No formal lifecycle, initialization, creates/affects/confers assertion, guaranteed
creation, process-to-quantity inherency rule, relationship restriction or equation
is introduced. No new quantity is declared. The body-quantity compositions used
in the private checks are schematic; their independently abstract quantity heads
are retained and no runtime instantiation or availability is inferred.
