# Region intended Areal semantics: steering clarification

On October23 the owner clarified that Region was intended as a convention supporting 2D analysis, as most GIS assets do; its volumetric description was wrong. Surface specificity is a realm choice rather than a subject choice. Elevation on a 2D support may inform analyses with 3D scope; 2.5D is a representation artifice and adds no worldview concept, identity, realm or subject.

`earth:Region is imod:Areal earth:Location` is unchanged. Geographic region and occupying material remain distinct. Realm entails a within-realm requirement; observational containment does not establish spatial enclosure, temporal coverage or mereology. No generic Surface realm is introduced. This is steering clarification, not a record of community-wide acceptance of experimental concepts.

The follow-on review and inclusion/holdout delta are in ../reviews/region-areal-20261023/README.md. Historical proposal receipts are preserved; remaining questions retain their original status unless explicitly superseded there.
