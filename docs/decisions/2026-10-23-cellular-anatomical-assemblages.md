# Cellular and anatomical assemblages — 2026-10-23

This experimental steering increment reuses the existing Life grouping contract. It is not recorded community acceptance. The owner accepted cell groupings under Assemblage, asking for meaningful intermediate articulation rather than a confusing jump to ecological groupings.

## Meaning and hierarchy

Assemblage remains an abstract Subject. Its wording now explicitly covers biological units observed as living individuals or anatomical structures; a structural specimen does not thereby assert current viability. AnatomicalAssemblage is an abstract Subject under it, with an anatomical location, arrangement or structural-continuity criterion supplying group unity. CellAssemblage is the concrete cellular-level specialization. No member-type, minimum-number, closed-membership, persistence or identity-transition axiom is added.

CellularBody is a concrete material Subject under physical:MaterialBody, distinct from the organism Agent reading. A living bacterium can remain an Individual for mass or movement observations without creating a mandatory second body observation. A separately targeted structural body/specimen can use CellularBody under its boundary/continuity criterion. Neither biological origin nor a prepared specimen proves current living activity.

An anatomical cell grouping and a grouping of whole organisms for ecological composition use different unity criteria. Population and Community remain their existing Agents; neither is reparented or used as a parent for this Subject branch. Abstractness remains declaration-local, so the abstract intermediate does not force CellAssemblage to be abstract. No new generic group root or universal anatomical/ecological partition is introduced.

## Deliberate exclusions

The optional association between structural and organism readings, configuration recognition/detection, new membership/adhesion relations, growth/differentiation convenience names, TissueBody/OrganBody and molecular individuation remain review-only. No creates, contains, sameAs, generic part-of or viability predicate is added. Single-cell organisms are not promoted to groups to fit syntax. Prior excluded research proposals remain preserved.

## Validation and independent review

Exact source hashes and final-byte results are recorded in ../reviews/cellular-hierarchy-20261023/VALIDATION.json and review.json. Independent review is recorded alongside them. Validation uses the existing bounded offline source/bean/reasoner/model-document harness and captured providers; it does not establish runtime execution, detection, deployment, physiological correctness of particular specimens or community approval. No software or ODO foundation change is part of this increment.
