# Seed biological genetic and herbivory processes - 2026-10-17

## Scope and source trail

Biology begins with Photosynthesis and MembraneTransport. Genetics begins with
DNAReplication and cellular DNA-to-RNA Transcription. Ecology adds Herbivory
without changing Population or Community. These processes neither assign a
new Agent/Subject reading to cells and organs nor settle gene, material, sequence
or group individuation. Biology and genetics remain free of an intrinsic Earth
restriction.

Photosynthesis is not restricted to plants, oxygen release or a single pathway.
Membrane transport crosses a biological membrane and is distinct from unrelated
bulk water flow. Replication is molecular template copying, not file duplication,
and does not assert a completed cell division or perfect fidelity. Transcription
is distinct from translation; functional noncoding products remain possible.
Herbivory means consumption of plant material; mortality alone is not feeding
evidence, nor does consuming a portion require the source plant to die.

Bootstrap anchors: biology-c06/c08 (CELL/MEMBRANE), genetics-c06/c07
(GLOSSARY/EXPRESSION), ecology-c07 (NPS). Their compartment, participant, effect,
rate and event proposals remain separate. No explicit part-of, cell agency,
gene-function identity or generic ecological group reparenting is introduced.
