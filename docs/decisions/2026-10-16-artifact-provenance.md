# Artifact production and modification - 2026-10-16

## Approved meanings

The owner approved abstract infrastructure:ArtifactProduction and abstract
infrastructure:ArtifactModification as broad families of physical:Activity.
Production brings a new artifact entity into existence under the model's
individuation criteria. Modification intentionally changes a continuing material
body, yielding or altering an artifact. Modification can make a natural body an
artifact without making it a new body.

A repair may modify a continuing artifact and produce a new component. Reuse of
an unchanged artifact need involve neither production nor modification. Agents,
material inputs, tools and outcomes are distinct participants; participation does
not alone imply incorporation or derivation. Unknown history remains unknown.
