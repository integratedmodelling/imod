# Living individuals and biological material - 2026-10-16

## Approved bounded foundation

The owner approved clarifying existing abstract Agent life:Individual as:
"An individual living organism, whose unity and continuity are established by the
biological criteria applicable to its kind."

The Agent declaration and deliberate agentic interpretation are retained.
Capacity for action does not require deliberation, human likeness or conscious
intention. Particular biological criteria and observations establish unity and
continuity; no universal physiology, reproduction or life-status test is imposed.
No separate Organism class, rename or alias is added.

The owner approved the compositional attribute life:OfBiologicalOrigin:
"Having material origin in living organisms."
It applies to existing physical:MaterialBody. Life imports physical for this bound;
physical continues to import imod, with no reverse Life dependency.

A living individual remains an Agent and an independently identified material
portion remains a Subject. They are not equated or double-typed. Biological
origin establishes neither current viability nor death, cultivation, harvest,
intended use or a formal Agent-body/part-of relationship. Mixtures and transformed
material require their own scope/provenance account.

Existing imod:Mass remains the mass quality. A biomass measurement identifies a
material pool, its boundary/inclusion criteria, units and measurement basis;
biological origin alone does not define that pool or supply a value.
