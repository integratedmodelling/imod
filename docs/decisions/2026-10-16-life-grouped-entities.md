# Grouped biological entities - 2026-10-16

## Approved provisional distinction

The owner approved retaining two independent abstract heads in Life for the
community strawman, while noting possible user confusion and allowing later
revision, merging of terminology or further articulation after review.

- Assemblage is a Subject: "An identifiable grouping of living individuals, with
  stated criteria for its unity and continuity."
- CollectiveAgent is an Agent: "An identifiable grouping of living individuals
  capable of acting as a unit."

Neither specializes the other. Core Agent-Subject disjointness is preserved;
the same grouped entity is not assigned both categories. Agent members do not
by themselves make a group an Agent, and collective agency does not make it one
life:Individual organism. No common parent that would impose both categories is
introduced. The names do not define every colloquial use of group or assemblage.

## Pragmatic agency and entity scope

The Agent articulation is an explicit worldview commitment at group level.
Internal interactions and intentions/planning where applicable can explain it,
but a mechanism, simulation or human-like intention is not required to annotate
an entity. Models supply dynamics. A vegetation association can be articulated
as a collective Agent without first modeling how every individual interacts.

A delimited grouping of plants described without a unit-level agency commitment
illustrates Assemblage. A vegetation association described as an acting unit
illustrates CollectiveAgent. These are semantic category choices, not automatic
inferences from co-location, a list of members or absence of a mechanism model.
The grouped entity can have its own qualities, relationships and evolution;
its kind supplies unity/continuity criteria. This adds no universal membership,
minimum cardinality, summation, part-of, persistence or identity-transition rule.

Configurations may describe recognition patterns; they do not replace the entity.
The earlier Configuration-first proposal is superseded for this primary grouping
representation. Both new heads remain independently abstract; identity
qualification does not erase that deliberate organizing choice.
