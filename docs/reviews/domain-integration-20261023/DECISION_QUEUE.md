# First cross-domain questions

Order reflects what each decision unblocks, not a proposed answer. All alternatives remain experimental; no community acceptance is recorded.

1. **Region dimensionality clarified by steering.** Region remains Areal and supplies a conventional geographic support for 2D/GIS analysis. The volumetric prose was wrong; no dimension-neutral or SurfaceRegion hierarchy is proposed. Surface is a realm choice, and 2.5D belongs to representation documentation. See [current clarification and delta](../region-areal-20261023/README.md). Historical snapshots remain evidence, not current questions. Material-water identity, generic land-unit individuation and configuration recognition remain open.

2. **Material water identity and canonical ownership.** Current `thing earth:WaterBody is earth:Aquatic earth:Region` identifies a geographic region. Held proposals are `thing hydrology:WaterBody is physical:MaterialBody` and `thing oceanography:MarineWaterBody is physical:MaterialBody`. Choose a shared material-water root with a marine specialization, or explicit separate material roots plus a stated bridge; distinguish both from geographic host. This unblocks groundwater, marine stocks, water quality native bearers and transfers. Do not infer equivalence from names, or equate material identity with region membership. See Earth hydrology/oceanography drafts and CROSS_DOMAIN_FINDINGS.

3. **Material individuation across chemistry and living bodies.** Held `thing chemistry:MolecularEntity is physical:MaterialBody`, `abstract thing genetics:DNAMolecule is physical:MaterialBody`, RNAMolecule and ChromosomeBody, plus `abstract thing life:CellularBody is physical:MaterialBody` (and TissueBody/OrganBody). Choose explicit material-body views with distinct bridges to existing biological Agent concepts, versus another individuating substantial head before adding shared ancestry. This unblocks molecular surface binding, transcript counts, biological membranes and cellular attachment. Current candidate PhasePortion/SolutionPortion does not settle constituent/mixture partitive inherency; that remains explicitly unresolved. Host/bearer, organism perspective and material constitution must remain distinguishable.

Settled constraints are not reopened: Ecosystem denotes biotic–abiotic coupling, EcosystemType is a separate typology identity hook, LandCover identity is legitimate and distinct from ecosystem typing, and observational containment is non-mereological and does not imply spatial/temporal coverage.
