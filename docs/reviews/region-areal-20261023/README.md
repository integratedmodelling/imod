# Region Areal clarification

Region keeps its existing Areal identity and geographic convention for 2D/GIS analysis. The volumetric prose was incorrect. Surface is a realm choice, not a new subject choice. 2.5D is representation documentation, not worldview vocabulary. This records steering clarification of intended semantics, not community-wide acceptance.

Three unchanged held meanings are admitted experimentally: hydrology:SurfaceCatchment, hydrology:CatchmentArea and geography:LocalRelief. Examples: a drainage footprint above a specified outlet is a SurfaceCatchment even in dry weather; an administrative boundary or groundwater recharge zone alone is not. CatchmentArea measures that delineated footprint, not stored water volume. LocalRelief measures maximum-minus-minimum ground elevation over the stated region, not elevation above datum or canopy height. Their source rationale, primary references and discriminating scenarios remain in the original Earth packet preserved under experiments/strawman-2026/review/domain-proposals-20261023/earth/PROPOSALS.md and SOURCES.md.

No new surface qualification is added: existing realm vocabulary provides no generic Surface realm, and SeaSurface/Crust/Pedosphere cannot be substituted universally. The generic LandSurfaceUnit and dependent fractions/management relation remain held for individuation and native support review. CoverMosaic retains its detection/recognition question. Material water and mixture partitive inherency remain unresolved. See REVIEW_RECORD.json for exact delta, declarations, IDs and hashes.
