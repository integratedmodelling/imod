Each row is a review address, not a commitment that the existing namespace merits a separate Tier-1 ontology. Retain all 23 existing domain addresses initially so no legacy area disappears. `imod` is root; `calendar` and `data` require a separate core/application-placement review. Each row label links to a compact candidate table with meanings, upper-derived categories, scope and counterexamples. Five organizing groups (material transformations; Earth systems; living systems; agency/collective organization; production/built systems/value) are editorial groupings only, never upper classes.

Legacy recovery means recover meaning after review, not copy obsolete syntax. Seeds in this table are proposals, not accepted declarations. ## Dependency rationale and overlap gates

The intended direction is root → generic domain articulation → specialized combinations → jargon aliases/models. Imports encode genuine semantic reliance, not all possible scientific interaction. Wildfire does not justify making atmosphere import ecology while ecology imports atmosphere. Cross-domain hazard, exposure and service examples should depend on the participating domains in a higher-tier bridge.

Before approving any row, test sibling dimensions for independent variation: biomass amount can vary with taxonomic identity fixed; management can vary with vegetation cover fixed; monetary value can vary with artifact identity fixed. Dependence is not orthogonality: water volume depends on its bearer; a relationship needs typed endpoints. A source's classifier may not have the identity principle of an observed body.

Proposed consolidations (unresolved): physical/physics, life/biology, society/sociology, agency/decision, economics/valuation. Retain both meanings where the distinction is supported, but do not retain namespaces merely for disciplinary prestige. Physical/Knowledge/Norms division remains D03–D04, separate from these practical ownership decisions.

Every legacy file, including incubation material and all nine im.aries sources, is in the source inventory. Incubation content is evidence of ideas, not accepted vocabulary. The im.aries water/carbon/pollination/flood/aesthetic/nca material should be assessed for reusable roles, beneficiaries, service flows and quantities, while equations, accounts and methods remain descriptions. Missing model functionality and missing runtime consequences must not be reported as missing Tier-1 vocabulary.
