## Review gates

1. Root/domain reviewers choose boundaries and correct upstream ambiguities with source evidence.
2. Dossier authors revise candidate alignments against exact accepted imports, retaining question failures and alternatives.
3. Actual parser, name/type validators, adaptation, loaded-worldview reasoner and representative models are separate checks. Most dossier terms are not declared, so parser success is only a lexical/syntactic result.
4. Humans approve exact proposal revisions, actions, base commits and hashes. No source confidence score, workflow stage or passing test constitutes approval.
