## Material findings

1. **HYD-R01: transported sediment is not concentration (blocking interpretation).** Q12 asks whether the next storm carries more sediment, but its expression resolves change in suspended-sediment concentration. Concentration can fall while transported mass rises. Retain the original narrative and record concentration as one component; require section discharge and interval-supported sediment mass/load, and distinguish suspended transport from bedload. This is a missing-observable/model gap, not permission to insert an equation into the ontology. USGS explicitly distinguishes concentration and loads and uses streamflow in their computation ([USGS sediment methods](https://water.usgs.gov/osw/suspended_sediment/time_series.html), accessed 2026-10-03, introductory method summary; not full method validation).
2. **HYD-R02: parent reference is not a verified scientific specialization (blocking ancestry).** In the supplied `src/earth.kwv`, WaterBody is `earth:Aquatic earth:Region`; Region is volumetric, and Aquatic descends from an EarthCover/ecoregion classification realm. LakeWaterBody/SpringWaterBody are defined as water itself; their proposed parent may describe an aquatic region rather than that material body. Carry the already acknowledged groundwater/reach ambiguity through these subjects and their bearer qualities. `earth:Waterway` is `earth:Riverine earth:Region`, not declared a WaterBody specialization. Applying a WaterBody-borne concentration directly to StreamReach therefore also has an unverified inherency path. Do not repair by silently declaring domain ancestry.
3. **HYD-R03: affects rationale overstates actual change (blocking binding justification).** The five process records repeat a rationale that transfer changes participating storage. A channel can sustain throughflow while its local storage remains constant; which body's storage is potentially affected must be identified. Context pack section on `affects` explicitly means potential change, not guaranteed net change. Rewrite to potential participant-specific storage effects and record missing donor/receiver bindings. Parameters such as infiltration flux for OverlandFlow may be relevant governing/contextual qualities without inhering in that process. Distinguish those relations instead of silently changing the quality bearer.
4. **HYD-R04: level versus storage (blocking meaning).** LakeDrawdownEpisode says fall of level/storage; those are related quantities, not interchangeable identity conditions. Fix the intended observation or retain two alternatives. Geometric changes and uncertain stage-volume relations make the distinction consequential. Head decline likewise cannot define groundwater volume depletion; the dossier already correctly records this negative case.
5. **METHOD-R01: raw count is not semantic coverage.** All five event candidates are blocked, yet coverage.shortfalls reports zero. This is accurate only as a count of brainstormed records. Report explored/grounded/semantically-ready/blocked counts separately; five names must not appear as a completed domain articulation. Preserve source confidence, scientific category fit, importance, grammar acceptance and semantic acceptance as different dimensions.
6. **METHOD-R02: stronger imported ancestry is user-specific.** Context pack section 3.5 permits Tier-1 keyword inheritance with optional explicit upper specialization. The user's current request mandates imported derivation. METHOD step 2 is appropriately stricter for this task, but should label its authority as the current user instruction rather than imply the unchanged production schema requires explicit parents universally.
7. **METHOD-R03: source-first does not mean source-supported or independent.** The retained question ledger explicitly discloses prior catchment exposure and metadata-only drought evidence; keep that disclosure. Question provenance is not complete semantic support. Several sources are broad introductory pages and cannot validate specialist event boundaries, groundwater body identity or all claimed governing parameters. A source/date/locator record is necessary but insufficient without claim-level scope.
   **Related ancestry requirement:** context pack section 7.4 explicitly prohibits adding a generic root alias merely to restate keyword inheritance. Dossier parent references to imod:Process/Event/Relationship must not be mechanically serialized as extra `is` axioms. Record foundational type separately as `implicit_type_inheritance`; meaningful imported domain specialization or an explicit upstream gap is needed under this user's stricter derivation request. A verified reference alone does not resolve this design tension.
8. **HANDOFF-R01: no API invention observed.** The handoff correctly calls fields local research metadata and proposed stage requirements, keeps context-pack 1.3 authoritative, and disclaims expert approval. Preserve that distinction when dossiers are automated. Ready-for-review should freeze hashes and import revisions and separately enumerate blocking versus deferred issues; approval must remain revision-specific and human.
