# valuation: domain bootstrap dossier

Status: research draft, not ready for executable promotion. No expert discussion or human approval has occurred. All names are provisional; all 15 question expressions are untested here until recorded parser results say otherwise. A parser result never validates scientific specialization.

Valuation begins by preserving disagreements. Accounting exchange value, an expressed ranking and a relationship to a place answer different questions. The same forest can be important under several perspectives without admitting one common scalar measure. Existing Asset as positive-valued substantial excludes zero/negative or non-cardinal judgments without justification, and current Hazard is an occurrent role, not a probability. Neither existing Risk/Vulnerability configurations nor implication comments count as implemented machinery. The proposed processes about appraisal are retained only as observed human activities; the computational appraisal procedure itself belongs outside this ontology.

## Quality summaries and predicates

- **ReportedPreference → Preferred/equally ranked/incomparable** (blocked): Potential partial ordering in one observer/comparison frame. Incomparability is explicit judgment, not missing data; no total-order axiom. No unknown/unmeasured/disputed predicate; preserve evidence state.
- **RiskTolerability → Acceptable/unacceptable under criterion** (blocked): Nominal judgment against stated loss/probability criterion and stakeholder frame. Boundaries supplied by contextual authority, not invented here. No unknown/unmeasured/disputed predicate; preserve evidence state.
- **ExchangeValue → Positive/zero/negative amount** (provisional): Ordered under matched valuation concept and monetary conventions. Does not define all-valued assets or a universal good/bad predicate. No unknown/unmeasured/disputed predicate; preserve evidence state.
- **DamageExtent → Damage severity classes** (blocked): Ordered only on an explicit damage dimension with declared thresholds; physical extent and monetary consequence are not interchangeable. No unknown/unmeasured/disputed predicate; preserve evidence state.

No generic Valuable, Vulnerable, Autonomous, Good or Resilient predicate is minted merely because the word is useful in conversation. Classification thresholds, comparisons and scopes must be supplied and reviewed. Schemes are not declared exhaustive or disjoint without evidence.
