# Climate–Nature–Economy review path through the dossiers

This is a set of linked review questions, not an executable causal chain. Exact expressions, evidence, cases and current parser status reside under the listed question IDs. No new expression below is claimed parser-tested. The earlier WILDFIRE.md remains an initial scenario; this path shows where the expanded vocabulary succeeds only partially or fails.

| Review step | Dossier probes | Meaning tested and remaining gap |
|---|---|---|
| Fire weather and transport | atmosphere-q12; physics-q04 | Surface pressure alone cannot determine smoke trajectory. Radiation transfer is a process, not thermal state; atmospheric circulation and plume models remain external. Do not infer a fire from weather categories. |
| Organism survival and recovery | life-q07/q08; biology-q14; ecology-q02; genetics-q14 | Seed presence does not imply germinability. Nondetection is not mortality; resprouting and a new seedling have different identity histories. Population abundance depends on a scoped population. Gene-expression association alone does not explain survival. |
| Land cover versus use | land-q11 | Fire can change observed cover while intended use persists. A scheme's burnt-area code is an authority assertion under that scheme, not universal equivalence to mortality, soil condition or land-use change. |
| Agricultural interventions after fire | agriculture dossier land-q08 and agriculture-q03/q04/q06 | Residue retention and actual grazing require occurrence evidence. Grazing rights, livestock ownership and raising responsibility differ; a practice or authority label does not prove sustainable condition. |
| Water and sediment | hydrology-Q01/Q08/Q12 | Surface catchment is not groundwater contributing area. High stage is not necessarily overbank flood. More transported sediment requires flux/load and contextual integration; concentration alone fails. Fire-to-runoff-to-erosion remains a testable model hypothesis, not an ontology implication. |
| Built assets and response | infrastructure-q01/q04/q09/q12/q14; engineering-q01/q15; agency-q03 | Physical suitability, structural condition, authorized use and actual service differ. Inspection is not guaranteed safety. Repair versus replacement requires identity criteria. A role conferred by an authorized occurrence and a subsequently detected shelter configuration are intended future semantics, not executed machinery. |
| Economic and social consequences | economics-q01/q03/q13; society-q01; sociology-q01; valuation-q02/q04/q10 | Income, production and asset destruction are different observables. Usual residence need not change through temporary evacuation. Trust is directed and context-dependent. Monetary exchange values do not exhaust loss or community values; viewpoint and comparability remain explicit. |

## A deliberately broken chain to reject

Burn-class label -> all organisms dead -> less infiltration -> flood -> infrastructure loss -> monetary damage is not licensed. Each arrow requires distinct occurrence evidence, quality changes, contextual models and potentially future detection machinery. A source class or an unanswered query must not supply missing links.

## Proposed review sequence

Hydrology and earth reviewers first settle catchment footprint, material water, channel persistence and typed transfer bearers. Soil/ecology reviewers inspect mechanisms as external models, keeping domain observables orthogonal. Infrastructure/agency reviewers distinguish physical connection from contingent service/authorization. Economics/valuation reviewers separate physical loss, account entries and plural value judgments. The context-pack proposal should contain only meanings that survive those reviews; backend exact-candidate acceptance must not auto-approve this scenario or claim model/application success.

At a time transition caused by a relevant occurrent, resolve change in the participating qualities separately. If some changes are unresolved, the twin runs with available knowledge and explicit unknowns. This document introduces neither zero-change inference nor state-retention rules.
