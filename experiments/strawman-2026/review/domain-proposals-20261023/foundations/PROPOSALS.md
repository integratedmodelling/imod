### Cross-domain acceptance matrix (future tests, not executed)

1. Contacting blocks at thermal equilibrium: PH1 positive, no unsupported positive PY3 transfer; reverse contact endpoints preserve bond identity.
2. Same parcel before/during passage through a fixed inlet: body mass pre-exists, moved amount is occurrence-specific; fixed region is not FluidParcel.
3. Crust-derived elevation of a terrestrial Region: no inherited Crust requirement merely from provenance; narrow Terrain application does not back-propagate through emergence.
4. Same measured number and unit for pressure/energy or pH/concentration: reject meaning equivalence; dimensions/numeric equality cannot replace native quantity/bearer semantics.
5. Filled cavity versus fluid occupant: host relation positive only for structural host, not occupant; observation container does not confer physical hosting.
6. Idle assembled pump: artifact/attachment can be observed; delivered flow, effectiveness and safety remain unknown until independently supported.
7. Local daily recurrence across a zone transition: preserve calendar convention; elapsed duration is separately evaluated, not fixed by the Daily label.
8. Mixed observer records: preserve explicit observed-as identity and actual observing Agent provenance separately; retrieving perspectives does not merge values or relax explicit restrictions.

## Unit and value-space review ledger

This is proposed evidence metadata, **not** invented executable `@unit` syntax or a new schema. Prefer supported existing mediator/model fields when eventually encoded. At minimum distinguish known dimensional unit, explicit unit one, unknown/not supplied, and nonnumeric/not applicable. Dimensionless does not mean meaningless or unknown; percentages, angles, logarithmic measures and ratios still need conventions. [S1]

## Primary literature and evidence sources

The unit ledger also uses [NIST SI units](https://www.nist.gov/pml/owm/metric-si/si-units), its base/derived-unit overview, as an independent national metrology reference for the unit families. All links below were browsed on 2026-10-23; descriptions state the use made of them. Scientific facts are distinguished above from this packet's ontology-design choices. IUPAC HTML access sometimes failed; primary search-index/plain/JSON results supplied the cited definitions. No paywalled text or invented quotations were used.

- **S1** [BIPM, The International System of Units, 9th ed., v4.01, June 2026](https://www.bipm.org/documents/d/guest/si-brochure-9-en-pdf), §§2.3, 5.4 and unit tables: quantities/units, amount, dimension one, numeric-value conventions. Does not dictate ODO categories.
- **S2** [Smith & Varzi (2000), Fiat and Bona Fide Boundaries, pp. 401–420](https://www.columbia.edu/~av72/papers/Ppr_2000.pdf), §1.1: material discontinuity, demarcation and mixed boundaries; supports individuation questions without importing their complete mereotopology.
- **S3** [IUPAC Gold Book, phase, P04528](https://goldbook.iupac.org/terms/view/P04528/plain): composition/state criterion; does not itself select a bounded observable Subject or persistence policy.
- **S4** [IUPAC, molecular entity, M03986](https://mail.goldbook.iupac.org/terms/view/M03986/plain): singular entity versus species ensemble; context-dependent precision.
- **S5** [NASA Glenn, Heat Transfer](https://www.grc.nasa.gov/www/k-12/airplane/heat.html): heat transfer differs from body state and depends on path. Simplified heat-capacity formula is not a universal axiom.
- **S6** [NASA Glenn, Newton's Second Law](https://www.grc.nasa.gov/WWW/K-12/BGP/newton2.html): directional mechanical quantities and momentum-change scope; classical mechanics only.
- **S7** [IUPAC, impedance, I02988](https://goldbook.iupac.org/terms/view/I02988/json): electrical complex impedance, evidence against treating every generic transport impedance as one scalar physical quantity.
- **S8** [IUPAC, diffusion, D01716](https://goldbook.iupac.org/terms/view/D01716/plain): molecular versus eddy transport, with atmospheric scope limits; not a universal constitutive law.
- **S9** [NASA, The Electromagnetic Spectrum](https://science.nasa.gov/ems/): electromagnetic wave propagation and interaction families; no ontology of all fields imported.
- **S10** [W3C/OGC, Time Ontology in OWL, 15 Nov 2022 Candidate Recommendation Draft](https://www.w3.org/TR/owl-time/), principles and temporal reference systems: positions/intervals/durations and calendar/clock alternatives. It is a draft, not a claim of an adopted k.LAB temporal ontology.
- **S11** [NASA Glenn, Speed of Sound Derivation](https://www.grc.nasa.gov/WWW/BGH/snddrv.html): small-disturbance propagation through gas, material/state dependence; no ideal-gas formula generalized to every medium.
- **S12** [IUPAC, amount concentration, A00295](https://mail.goldbook.iupac.org/terms/view/S06072): constituent amount divided by mixture volume; canonical DOI `10.1351/goldbook.A00295` is identified on that returned primary page.
- **S13** [IUPAC, mass fraction, M03722](https://www.old.goldbook.iupac.org/plain/M03722-plain.html): constituent mass over total mixture mass.
- **S14** [IUPAC, pH, P04524](https://mail.goldbook.iupac.org/terms/view/P04524): hydrogen-ion activity and measurement conventions; not a concentration shortcut.
- **S15** [IUPAC, precipitation, P04795](https://goldbook.iupac.org/terms/view/P04795/json): chemical, electrostatic and meteorological senses are explicitly separate.
- **S16** [IUPAC, adsorption, A00155](https://mail.goldbook.iupac.org/terms/view/A00155/plain): interface enrichment; not universal dissociation or bulk absorption.
- **S17** [NIST Dataplot, Standardize](https://www.itl.nist.gov/div898/software/dataplot/refman2/auxillar/standard.htm): z-score versus range transformation and reference-group dependence; ontology naming/degenerate-case policies remain local decisions.
- **S18** [Taylor & Kuyatt, NIST Technical Note 1297, 1994](https://www.nist.gov/pml/nist-technical-note-1297), §§2–7 and [NIST uncertainty definitions](https://physics.nist.gov/cuu/Uncertainty/basic.html?level=1): standard/expanded uncertainty and evaluation bases, distinct from missingness.
