## 2. Foundation arbitration and inherited contracts

1. **D:** `life:Individual` is an abstract Agent; unicellular organisms qualify. `life:Assemblage` (Subject) and `life:CollectiveAgent` (Agent) are independent, provisionally accepted heads. Do not dual-type a grouped referent or automatically reparent Population/Community. A configuration can recognize a group without replacing its substantial.
2. **D:** Species is Identity only. TAXA currently has `datasetKey:312578`, species/genera codelists. Classification does not prove living presence, individual boundaries or interaction. A species-named food-web node must denote an observed population/resource qualified by that identity, never the Identity itself.
3. **D/Q:** Root Relationship is neutral; independently inherit structural/functional and directed/bond. Abstract AgentInteraction may fix functional nature while leaving orientation open. Its source is agentic; its target may be nonliving. Do not impose an all-Agent target at this intermediate. Root Network's “subjects” wording should be reviewed for Agent-inclusive meaning, not silently rewritten.
4. **D:** Quality/process native bearer is not observation containment. Containment neither asserts physical parts nor geometry, coverage, extents, existence or members. Realm means a within-realm requirement, not an ordinary habitat label. `observed as` is an explicit semantic perspective restriction; default runtime observer preference does not rewrite stored meanings.
5. **D/Q:** Configuration `of` means emergence; relationships supply Network emergence, never quality inherency. Detection establishes configurations. `model each Configuration` is illegal; resolving an emerged configuration is distinct. Current context pack records model validation and trigger registration, but incomplete detection/catalog/placement and empty `emergentResolvables`. No automatic network/group lifecycle, population aggregation, food transfer or ecosystem creation is claimed.
6. **Q:** The active Community emergence clause is too weak to encode interaction-grounded community boundary. Biological group decisions allow unit-level agency without detailed mechanism modeling. Reconcile recognition criteria with annotation of already identified Agents; do not require every organism interaction to have been simulated before annotation.
7. **Q:** `agency:Participant` has disjoint Volitional/Patient/Experiencer. Its Patient prose disallows perception, and its Volitional prose binds agency to conscious initiation. These are scoped roles, not a partition of all biological agency. Review overlap across different participation contexts before retaining disjointness. `Decision` process/event mismatch and machine-oriented `Assessment` remain active contradictions.
8. **Q:** `physical:Growth` includes “positive or negative growth” and functional throughput under Transformation. A biological size-increase process is narrower and should not inherit that mixed wording without a focused review. `physical:Homeostasis` exists: reuse a reviewed specialization rather than invent a parallel generic Maintenance head.
