# Bounded evidence for four domain gaps

## Physics: thermophysical foothold

**Evidence and scope.** [NIST TN 2312, Burgess (2025), abstract](https://www.nist.gov/publications/phase-transition-temperatures-solid-elements-atmospheric-pressure) evaluates transitions among crystalline forms of elements at atmospheric pressure. It supports distinguishing material composition, phase and transition, within that scope. The [NIST TDE pure-compound property list](https://trc.nist.gov/TDE/Help/TDE103b/How_to_do_Evaluations/Evaluation_for_Pures/ListPureCompound-Properties.htm), single-valued and temperature/pressure-dependent property sections, separates temperature, density, viscosity and other properties; its phase/condition qualifications matter. This software documentation supplies a bounded property inventory, not a worldview taxonomy.

**Proposed distinctions.** Review a material body/sample as bearer; phase arrangement as a candidate configuration of that material; transition as process (or bounded event under an explicit episode perspective); and temperature, density and viscosity as dependent qualities. A transition temperature is not automatically the current temperature of a sample. These category assignments are interpretations, not claims that NIST endorses ODO categories.

**Prohibited inference and limits.** The same elemental composition does not determine phase or all quality values. A phase label does not instantiate a transition process. Do not emit universal temperature-to-transition consequences from this evidence. This foothold covers neither general mechanics, electromagnetism nor quantum theory; even general phase articulation needs broader review. Phase-as-configuration versus a qualifying predicate remains open.

## Oceanography: material, body, circulation and condition

**Evidence and scope.** [NOAA-hosted Ocean Literacy 3.2 (January 2024)](https://cdn.oceanservice.noaa.gov/oceanserviceprod/education/literacy/ocean-literacy-english.pdf), Principles 1A–C/E (PDF page 7) and 5D–F (page 10), distinguishes ocean/basins, seawater properties, circulation, organisms and ecosystems. It is an educational synthesis, not a set of universally applicable causal axioms. [TEOS-10 manual at the supplied v3_04 URL](https://www.teos-10.org/pubs/gsw/v3_04/pdf/TEOS-10_Manual.pdf), section 2.5 and appendices A.4–A.5, distinguishes salinity measures and their composition-sensitive relationships. Operational conversion belongs to descriptions, not an unrestricted equality axiom.

**Proposed distinctions.** Separate seawater as material/composition from an identifiable water body and the basin containing it; distinguish circulation process from the body. Treat salinity and dissolved oxygen concentration as different dependent qualities of an explicit water bearer. A marine ecosystem is a candidate ecological configuration with organism/environment scope, not an alias for seawater or basin.

**Prohibited inference and limits.** Practical Salinity and Absolute Salinity are not unrestricted `equals` aliases. Salinity alone does not establish oxygen concentration or ecosystem condition. Educational circulation narratives do not license new `affects`/`creates` clauses. These sources do not complete circulation, marine chemistry or ecosystem vocabulary, and do not resolve every body/basin boundary.

## Genetics: material arrangement, expression and function

**Evidence and scope.** [NHGRI Talking Glossary, supplied 2022 PDF](https://www.genome.gov/sites/default/files/media/files/2022-05/NHGRI-Talking-Glossary.pdf), Allele (page 1), DNA (6), Gene/Gene–Environment Interaction/Gene Expression (9), and Locus (14), provides introductory distinctions among sequence variants at genomic locations, DNA and regulated expression. Its human/diploid examples must not become universal organism axioms. The [NHGRI Gene entry, definition and narration](https://www.genome.gov/genetics-glossary/Gene), explicitly recognizes debate and a broader reading including genes for noncoding RNA.

**Proposed distinctions.** Distinguish organism/cell bearer, DNA material or segment, and nucleotide arrangement borne by that material. A written sequence is a representation of that arrangement; the arrangement is not automatically assigned to Knowledge. Review an allele as an alternative sequence at a corresponding locus. Separate expression process, sequence, product, product activity and phenotype. Arrangement-as-configuration versus another dependent structural reading remains open, as does gene scope and functional individuation.

**Prohibited inference and limits.** Sequence alone does not entail expression, active product or phenotype in a context. A protein-only gene definition would erase the broader source reading. Any `creates RNA` clause still needs explicit scope and support; none is added. The sources do not cover all inheritance, regulation, genomic diversity or gene-boundary controversies. Experimental assays and sequence-analysis algorithms remain models/methods.

## Sociology: household relations and work relations

**Evidence and scope.** [UNECE 2030 census recommendations, chapter 16](https://w3.unece.org/recs2030census/webpage19.html), section 16.2.2.1 paragraphs 958–963, distinguishes housekeeping and household-dwelling approaches; its boarder/lodger example uses meals and facilities. Paragraphs 979–981 delimit family nuclei and extended families using specified relationships and household scope. These are census definitions, not universal definitions of family. The [ILO ICSE-18 manual](https://webapps.ilo.org/ilostat-files/Documents/ICSE-18_manual.pdf), pages 4–8, overview and “Two dimensions” discussion, separates persons/jobs/work activities/economic units and authority/economic-risk classification dimensions.

**Proposed distinctions.** Separate co-residence, shared domestic provision and specified biological, adoptive or partnership relations. Shared provision is a review dimension motivated by the census examples, not a claim that UNECE requires pooled finances. For work, separate performance of activity, authority over it, remuneration and economic risk. Review these as scoped relations, participation roles and dependent qualities; do not make statistical categories intrinsic human kinds.

**Prohibited inference and limits.** Co-residence establishes neither kinship nor shared finances. Remuneration alone does not establish employee legal status. Census and labour-statistical categories require scheme/jurisdiction/reference-period context and do not settle legal rights. These footholds do not cover sociology as a whole, institutional theory, power, culture or all forms of social organization. The society/sociology boundary remains unresolved.
