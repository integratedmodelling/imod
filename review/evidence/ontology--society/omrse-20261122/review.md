# Society under review

**Decision requested.** Should Society distinguish the occasion on which an organization admits a member from the membership that continues afterwards? A clinic can be a member of a federation today even if its admission date is unknown. Conversely, minutes can record a successful June vote admitting the clinic effective July 1. Counting the June vote as a June membership changes the result of a historical roster query. The [proposal](../../../proposals/ontology--society/20261124T083132Z--git-10d00cb4b206--society-omrse-r1/proposal.yaml) offers one event with this effective-time boundary. Reviewers can accept the distinction, prefer dated relationship observations, or move admission to a specialist governance vocabulary.

This is a Tier 1 review for the Society maintainers, with Sociology, Economics and census-method reviewers. The existing project README identifies the tier. The 2026-11-22 live capture used Resources project selection and two identical Reasoner reports around the harvest. All 26 ontology URN/hash pairs matched. The workspace matched all 26 sources and both strategy texts after accounting for line endings. The accepted report identifies worldview `_uu_wv:7098827169982474`, knowledge revision 1, consistent, with no worldview diagnostics. The file hashes, providers, commits and transitive imports are in the proposal. The report gives direct inferred ODO CollectiveAgent for Organization and StructuralRelationship for OrganizationMembership. Membership direction and endpoint types are authored clauses. These checks establish the baseline only.

## Where the external meanings transfer

**Organization and assemblage.** OMRSE reuses OBI:0000245. Its rules and membership account helps ask who can act *on behalf of* a unit. The released class sits under BFO material entity, and its editor note records disagreement about that placement. IMOD's approved criterion is capacity for action as a unit. Neither test alone decides the continuity of a merger, split or successor organization. Ask for a case where the name and staff persist but a new organization takes over, and another where all officers change but the unit continues. The historical Institution and SocialAgent names were retired by explicit decision. [Release, OBI:0000245](http://purl.obolibrary.org/obo/OBI_0000245) · [IMOD organization decision](../../project--imod/editor-records/2026-10-16-organization.md)

Three readings of “the clinics in this district” matter. A district filter returns records. A maintained network may have its own continuity criterion and qualify under `imod:CollectiveSubject`. A federation empowered to negotiate can qualify under `society:Organization`. Human component identity alone, as restricted in OMRSE:00000023, supplies no social unity test. OMRSE:00000267 adds demarcation by a categorization scheme. OMRSE:00000033 gives the particularly useful test of an aggregate composed of organizations without itself being an organization. IMOD's `life:Assemblage` is biological; `life:SocialGroup`, `ecology:Community` and `ecology:Population` already take a collective-Agent reading. A list of residents justifies none of those specializations. An observed network pattern could support a configuration and a subsequent group model. Its detection alone establishes no organized Agent. [OMRSE release](http://purl.obolibrary.org/obo/omrse/releases/2026-09-03/omrse.owl) · [Collective lineage](../../project--imod/editor-records/2026-11-06-collective-lineage.md)

**Recognized ties and organizational parts.** OBI:0000846 has organization range and an inverse. Its definition invokes a member role. IMOD gives membership its own countable observable and permits any qualifying Agent at source. Reverse lookup answers “which members?” without adding an inverse domain concept. The relationship has no transitivity clause. An affiliated clinic, an employed clinician and a department that lacks separate membership can all appear on the same organization chart. Membership covers the first only if recognized; employment fits the second; administrative inclusion remains an open question for the third. Review whether the unit can itself act, whether it survives reorganization, and whether an organizational-unit relation is needed. The earlier decision deferred general formal parthood. [Membership property](http://purl.obolibrary.org/obo/OBI_0000846) · [Organization decision](../../project--imod/editor-records/2026-10-16-organization.md)

**Households.** The OMRSE housing-unit reading fits a US census or synthetic-population dataset and admits a singleton. A housekeeping survey can distinguish two independently provisioning families under one roof, or identify shared provision among people lacking a conventional dwelling. For a dwelling-based count, identify the unit and classify its occupants under that survey's rule. For a housekeeping count, identify the living arrangement and its continuity under shared provision. Keep both readings available. An immediate subclass of CollectiveSubject needs a defensible account of what persists when only one person remains. An alternative is to model household affiliation to an identifiable living arrangement, whose participant count can vary. `infrastructure:Building` cannot identify units inside a multi-unit building by itself. The householder role identifies a designated survey reference person under particular rules; it supplies no family authority, ownership by every member, or need for evacuation assistance. [Housing unit and household](https://mcwdsi.github.io/OMRSE/modeling/Housing-unit-and-Household/) · [Householder](https://mcwdsi.github.io/OMRSE/modeling/Householder/)

**Roles, offices and mandates.** In OMRSE a role can inhere in a person or organization. OMRSE:00000086 identifies a human role arising from an organizational declaration. The modeling guide considers participation in a conferring process and a separate dependent output of a declaration. IMOD can already classify a worker in context and name the employing agent. A further test is a treasurer who serves two associations, with different signing powers in each. A bare officer role loses the counterparty. Two membership edges lose the powers, and either association might appoint a nonmember. Compare (a) an authorized-to-represent relationship with organization, scope and interval and (b) a persistent office with appointments to it. A vacant office distinguishes these alternatives. Before proposing a class, obtain a vacancy case, acting appointment and delegated mandate with explicit rules. `agency:Participant` concerns participation in an occurrence. The imprecise gloss on `agency:SocialObject` mentions law and money while the declaration is an abstract thing; deriving all institutional roles or offices from it would resolve none of these bearer questions. [Role conferral discussion](https://mcwdsi.github.io/OMRSE/modeling/What-is-the-relation-between-a-role-and-the-organization-that-creates-sanctions-confers-it/) · [OMRSE overview](https://mcwdsi.github.io/OMRSE/modeling/OMRSE-Overview/)

**Processes and events.** OMRSE's imported social act (IAO:0021003) requires a planned, directed process involving perception. Deontic declaration (IAO:0021009) narrows this to creation or revocation of a role; a document act is a further case. Those restrictions would be too strong for social interaction in general. IMOD can track negotiation, commitment, adoption of a rule, effective admission and its recording separately. Proposed admission is a bounded functional event under `imod:Event`. The sketch names its parent, while a future occurrence model must bind agent, organization and effective criterion. There is no generic inference from a declaration to successful membership. For founding, dissolution, resignation and expulsion, first establish which rule changes organizational identity or membership and whether the source distinguishes the transition from discovery of the change. Legal registration may precede or follow an organization beginning to act. [Released social act](http://purl.obolibrary.org/obo/IAO_0021003) · [Deontic declaration](http://purl.obolibrary.org/obo/IAO_0021009)

## Dependency and migration decisions

Retain the Society imports of IMOD, Life, Agency, Earth and Physical. Existing generalized collective roots suffice. Keep the biological scope of Life and Ecology. Keep Sociology's generic social connection between agents and the narrower economic work relationships at their current addresses. Society membership could be understood as a social relation, but declaring `is sociology:SocialRelation` in Society would create a reverse import. If cross-domain retrieval by that parent becomes a requirement, compare moving the shared parent to Society with a downstream alignment in Sociology. Moving it would require migrating `sociology:WorkRelation`, `sociology:ProvidesSupportTo`, inherited economic consumers and external references. Also test whether the proposed parent accidentally includes residence, whose target is a location.

Only Economics, Land and Sociology import Society directly or transitively in this checkout. Economics and Sociology explicitly use HumanIndividual; Land imports Society without a qualified Society reference. Neither strategy names Society. The generic CONNECTION strategy resolves endpoint collections for model discovery; a resolved collection has no automatic institutional identity. An additive event avoids reference migration. Narrowing the member endpoint to humans would lose federations. Moving organizations under biological groupings would change ancestry queries and the meaning of nonbiological members. No upstream edit is justified by the admission example.
